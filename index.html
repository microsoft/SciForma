Abstract

Structural fidelity is essential to scientific methodology diagrams. These diagrams must faithfully render components, directional relations, and textual annotations. Since a single error, such as a reversed arrow or an unreadable equation, can invalidate the entire figure, structural fidelity is inherently conjunctive: correctness on one axis cannot compensate for failure on another.

We introduce SciForma, a framework that decomposes diagram quality into three structural axes, Component, Arrow, and Text, guided by a structural inventory. We curate SciFormaData-700K for structured training and SciFormaBench-2K for logic-verified evaluation, and develop M-DPO to enforce simultaneous correctness across all axes. SciForma-9B exceeds all open-source baselines and GPT-Image-1.5 on SciFormaBench-2K and AIBench.

SciFormaData-700K

Structural Inventory. SciForma formalizes diagrams with three universal primitives: Component (entities), Arrow (topology), and Text (annotations). These form the structural inventory — a ground-truth checklist shared by training captions and benchmark rubrics. Correctness is conjunctive: all three axes must be satisfied simultaneously.

726K methodology diagrams from 593K arXiv sources (2015–2025), each paired with a structural-inventory caption (547 tokens avg). Editing triplets (70K) are SAM3-grounded, OpenAI-o3-verified edits of 1–3 structural attributes. SciFormaBench-2K: 2,000 human-verified instances across Simple / Medium / Hard, judged by GPT-5.4.

Data construction pipeline: PDF extraction → diagram detection → structured caption generation → quality filtering → editing augmentation.

Method

SciForma training pipeline overview.

Two-Stage SFT. SciForma fine-tunes FLUX.2-klein-base-9B in two stages. Stage 1 trains on the full 661K generation pairs at 768px and 1024px resolution. Stage 2 focuses on 244K high-quality pairs with richer structural complexity. All captions are structural inventories encoding Component, Arrow, and Text axes.

M-DPO: Multi-Dimensional Conjunctive Preference Optimization. One winner vs. three axis-specific losers (C, A, T) per sample. A multi-way Bradley–Terry objective enforces conjunctive correctness: the update is positive only when the winner beats every loser simultaneously. Adaptive gradient reweighting concentrates effort on failing axes.

M-DPO ablation on SciFormaBench-2K. Text axis benefits most (+3.16 pts).

  • Conjunctive objective. Correct only when all three axes improve simultaneously.
  • Adaptive gradient focus. Failing axes receive concentrated gradient; satisfied axes near-zero.
  • Multi-way Bradley–Terry. Equivalent to InfoNCE with D axis-anchored hard negatives.
  • Strict DPO generalization. Reduces to standard DPO when D = 1.

Iterative Refinement via Editing

Closed-loop editor: defect detection → priority-ranked edit plan → SciForma-9B targeted inpainting → global verification. Loop terminates when the structural inventory is satisfied.
