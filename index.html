SciFormaData-700K & SciFormaBench

We build a large-scale data engine spanning 338,214 arXiv papers (2015–2025), producing 661K generation pairs and 70K editing triplets. The dataset is organized around a structural primitive: a per-axis inventory of components, arrows, and text labels. SciFormaBench-2K provides 2,000 human-verified benchmark instances across three difficulty levels, evaluated by a GPT-5.4 judge along the same three axes.

Data construction pipeline: PDF extraction → diagram detection → structured caption generation → quality filtering → editing augmentation.

Structural inventory: from structural primitives (components / arrows / text labels) to a hierarchical per-axis rubric used for both training and evaluation.

Method

SciForma training pipeline overview.

Two-Stage SFT. SciForma is built on FLUX.2-klein-base-9B and fine-tuned in two stages. Stage 1 trains on the full 661K generation pairs across diverse resolution tracks (768px and 1024px), establishing a broad layout and structural understanding. Stage 2 focuses on a high-quality 244K subset with richer structural complexity, sharpening the model's ability to faithfully reproduce component topology and text alignment.

M-DPO. After SFT, we apply Multi-Dimensional Conjunctive Preference Optimization. For each training sample we construct a multi-way preference group: one winner and three axis-specific losers targeting Component, Arrow, and Text independently. A Bradley–Terry objective enforces simultaneous correctness across all axes, while adaptive gradient reweighting routes optimization effort toward the weakest axis at each step. This breaks the SFT plateau where scalar DPO and GRPO stagnate.
